Happy Birthday Jo!

A personal letter · Happy Birthday

Dear Jo,

I don't know where to begin, but there are a lot of things I have wanted to tell you hehe. Some of these things might sound random and some might make you wonder how I even noticed them… but honestly, I pay attention to more things about you than you probably think. I notice the little details, even the ones you might consider unimportant and not that deep. I notice the way you act, the way you talk, the things you enjoy, and even the little changes in your mood. I might not always say something about them, but that doesn't mean I don't notice.

I know some of your favorite songs, and sometimes I remember them because I associate them with you; some are in my playlist right now. I notice how much you enjoy eating sweet candies, especially the kinds of sweets you'd normally expect little kids to love. I find those little things amusing, and somehow, they make you more interesting to me. I also notice how you enjoy having someone to talk to while working on your pending activities or projects. Even when you're busy accomplishing something, you seem to appreciate having someone around to share conversations with and enjoy playing your favorite songs while you're working. Those little habits tell me more about you than you might realize.

But beyond those small and funny details, there are things about you I notice on a deeper level.

I notice how hard you try to prove that you are capable of doing everything on your own… you seem determined to show people that you don't need anyone's help, just someone around to talk to, and that you are strong enough to handle whatever comes your way. I respect your independence, and I admire how much effort you put into the things you need to accomplish. But sometimes, I wonder if you have become so used to handling everything alone because you feel like you don't have anyone to talk to when life becomes too messy.

I hope you understand that being independent doesn't mean you have to carry every burden by yourself. You don't always have to prove yourself to other people. You are allowed to ask for help, someone you can rely on. You are allowed to tell someone when you're tired. You are allowed to admit that something is becoming too much for you. There is nothing embarrassing about needing someone, and there is nothing wrong with allowing other people to be there for you. You don't have to be strong every single day because there is always tomorrow.

I also notice how quickly your attitude changes whenever you feel disrespected, ignored, or unappreciated. I understand why those things affect you because I know that you are a soft-hearted person and I can feel that every time we talk. Nobody deserves to feel small, especially when they have done their best to treat others properly. You deserve respect just as much as every other girl deserves respect. Nobody has the right to harass you, make you uncomfortable, or treat you as if your feelings don't matter. Please learn to stand up for yourself and speak out whenever someone crosses your boundaries. You don't have to tolerate disrespect just to keep the peace. If someone makes you feel unsafe, don't hesitate to seek help from people you trust or share those situations with me. Your safety and dignity should always come first.

At the same time, I hope you also learn when to step back and control your reactions. I know you have your reasons for feeling upset, and I'm not saying you should ignore your feelings. What I'm trying to point out is that sometimes, the way you respond to certain situations matters too. You don't have to let every disrespectful person affect your peace of mind. There are situations where walking away is better than arguing, and there are moments when explaining yourself isn't even worth your energy. You deserve to protect your peace without having to hurt yourself emotionally in the process, and I know that because I once saw you making the “cold and silent treatment.”

There's also something I want you to understand about the way you handle responsibilities. I know you want to help your friends, and I know you sometimes take on things because you don't want to disappoint anyone. But please remember that their responsibilities are still their responsibilities. Their projects and pending activities should not automatically become your burden. Helping someone is a good thing, but allowing people to depend on you for everything is different. You need to learn how to set boundaries. You don't have to say yes every time someone asks for help. You don't have to sacrifice your own time, energy, and responsibilities just to make things easier for other people. Some people might get too comfortable depending on you, and without realizing it, you might end up being taken advantage of. Learn to recognize when someone genuinely needs your help and when someone is simply passing their responsibilities to you.

And please, learn to give yourself some rest.

I know how you sometimes keep working even when you're already tired, thinking about the activities you haven't finished, the projects you still need to accomplish, and the things waiting for you tomorrow. But I hope you realize that your health matters more than a deadline. Those pending activities can still be worked on tomorrow. You don't have to finish everything in one sitting, especially when your body is already telling you to take some rest. Take a break when you need one. Get enough sleep, eat properly, and take medicine if you're not feeling alright. Please don't neglect yourself just because you have responsibilities to finish. Schoolwork is important, but your health should never be something you sacrifice for it.

I know I'm telling you a lot of things you should change, and I hope you don't misunderstand my intentions. I'm not saying these things because I think you're difficult or because I want you to become someone else. I know you have imperfections, and I know there are things about yourself you're still trying to understand. I have my own imperfections too. None of us has everything figured out. I hope you learn to treat yourself with the same consideration you give to other people. Be patient with yourself. Learn when to say no, when to ask for help, and when to rest. You don't have to change everything overnight. Take your time.

And if there is one thing I want you to remember after reading this letter, it's that you don't have to face everything alone.

If you ever need someone to talk to, someone to listen to your stories, or someone you can rely on when things become overwhelming, just reach out to me or to Carl. You don't have to think twice about sharing what's bothering you. You can tell me about your problems, your frustrations, your accomplishments, your thoughts, or even the smallest things that happened during your day. I'll listen. Even if you repeat the same story several times, even if your thoughts are all over the place, or even if you don't know how to explain what you're feeling, I'll still listen.

And you don't have to pretend to be okay whenever we talk. You don't have to make every conversation interesting or hide the things you're struggling with. Sometimes, you can simply talk without worrying about what the other person might think. I might not always have the right words to say, same with Carl, and I might not have solutions to every problem you tell me about, but I can offer you my time and attention. Sometimes, having someone who listens without judging is enough.

I know we're only friends, and I understand where we stand. Carl sometimes gets a little hard to understand simply because of his childhood and past traumas, but I'm not writing this letter to make you feel obligated to respond in a certain way, nor am I expecting you to change your feelings because of what I've written. I simply want you to know how I see you and how much I appreciate the person you are, including the little things you might not even notice about yourself.

You are worth appreciating, even with all your imperfections. You don't have to be perfect to deserve kindness, respect, patience, and genuine care. You're a human being and you're allowed to make mistakes, have bad days, change your mind, and learn from your experiences. You don't need to accomplish everything to prove your worth. You don't need to please everyone to be appreciated. And you certainly don't need to become someone else just to deserve the love and respect you should already receive.

I hope you become kinder to yourself. I hope you find people who make you feel comfortable enough to express what you're going through. I hope you learn that asking for help doesn't make you weak and that resting doesn't make you irresponsible. Most of all, I hope you never forget that you deserve the same care you continuously give to others.

I don't know what the future holds, and I don't know how things will turn out between us. But for now, I'm grateful to know you. I'm grateful for the conversations, the little moments, and even the ordinary things I've come to associate with you. I'll continue appreciating the person you are and I hope you continue becoming a better version of yourself, not because other people expect you to, but because you deserve a life where you feel happy and respected.

Take care of yourself, okay? Not only when things become difficult, but even on ordinary days. Eat properly, get some rest, don't push yourself too hard, and remember to smile whenever you find a reason to. And whenever life feels a little too heavy, remember that there's someone willing to listen to your stories.

And lastly, happy birthday! I hope you enjoy every moment of your special day. I hope you get to spend it with the people who make you happy, laugh until your stomach hurts, and make memories you'll look back on with a smile. May this year bring you unexpected blessings, new experiences, and opportunities you've been waiting for.

I hope you get to do more of the things you love, visit places you've always wanted to go, try things you've never experienced, and collect memories worth keeping. I also hope you receive the kind of happiness you don't have to question and the kind of friendships where you never have to pretend to be someone else.

You deserve a birthday filled with genuine smiles and a year filled with good things. I hope your wishes come true, even the ones you keep to yourself.

Enjoy your day. Don't forget to treat yourself, eat your favorite food, and, of course, buy yourself those sweet candies you love.

Once again, happy birthday! I'm glad our paths crossed, and I'm grateful to have you in my life, even if we're only friends.

Sincerely, Haiverson
